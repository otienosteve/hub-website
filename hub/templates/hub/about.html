Our key priorities at Agentic AI Hub

At Agentic AI Hub, we believe Africa has the talent and potential to become a global leader in AI innovation. To achieve this, we focus on five key priorities:

Data

Promoting access to relevant, high-quality data that reflects African contexts and addresses local challenges.

Compute

Supporting access to affordable computing resources that enable AI development and innovation.

Models

Encouraging the development and adaptation of AI models that serve African needs and solve real-world problems.

Skills

Equipping African talent with practical AI engineering skills to build, deploy, and maintain intelligent applications.

Agentic AI

Accelerating the development and adoption of AI agents that can reason, use tools, and perform tasks to solve real-world problems.
